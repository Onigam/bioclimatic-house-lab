# Lessons of the Bioclimatic House lab

Seven lessons, two questions each, every one opening on a scenario of the simulation and naming others, so that together they go through all twelve (`src/data/lessons.ts`). The heart is the second (the slow wave, the lab's backbone: the textbook finds the inventors' own measurements) and the fifth (the film's question). What the model finds is used in place of the research's hand figures, and where it does not retrieve a source the lesson says so.

## What the lessons say about the model

« Never tuned »: the flow (34–41 L/s against about 20), the split between air and conduction (51 against 31 %), the inner face 2 K low, the winter months, the reverse flow are all said as disagreements. The answer to the film's question is « a good part, depending on who counts, and what », with the ladder 32, 42.5, 47 % and what it takes to reach 61 %. Lesson 7 carries the earth tube's hygiene and the radon line once (a test counts them), and the architect's opinion without his numbers.

## Tests

`src/data/lessons.test.ts`: seven lessons, fourteen questions, two each, three choices at least, the answer in range and not always the same, every scenario named as the panel names it in both languages, every part standing in the world it opens, each paragraph under 900 characters, and what each lesson must say.

## After the fact-check

Lesson 2 says the wet-concrete gap has two causes in the paper and that the model retrieves neither as a gap; it presents « the noon sun arrives when the house sleeps » as the lab's reading (the paper says « too long »), and the quiz's question says so. Lesson 3 attributes 36.7 % to the paper's own rows. Lesson 4 gives the June flow with its caveat (the paper does not say its flow was the summer mode). Lesson 5 says the sun « can save » 60 to 70 %, gives the colleague's 75 to 90 % for houses of that kind in France, and the 1978 simulation as unread; the scenario « La même maison, mieux isolée » replaces « Isoler d'abord ». Lesson 6 no longer says « à vous de choisir ». Lesson 7 gives the earth-tube architect as « transcribed roughly » and treats radon as physics (a ground gas that can collect in spaces in contact with the earth, a buried pipe included; an airtight pipe and a measurement where it is a concern), with the neutral line that rules differ by country and site; it cites no zone and no national text.
