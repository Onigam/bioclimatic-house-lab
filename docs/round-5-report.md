### R4-1. What the cover reads now, and what it does at 07:50, 08:55 and 09:40

The old reading was the state's irradiance against the clear sky's, taken only with the sun 11° or more up and held below. The model's ridge (5.7°) takes the beam off a clear morning, so the dawn could not be read, and the sky kept the day before's cover through the night: the 24th's dawn was the 23rd's blue, and flipped to grey in 24 frames at 09:00; and the clear 25th's dawn was the dull evening's grey, which turned blue at 11°.

**The recorded day's own share.** `dayShare` (`src/three/sun.ts`) is the recorded table's total for the state's date (`state.recordDate`, « 24/12 »; the table is `RECORDS` of `sim/climate`, which the lab already reads in `drawings/pl07-christmas.ts` and the panel) over the clear day's at the site (`clearDay`, the engine's own division, its `k`): **0.056 on the 24th, 1.06 on the 23rd, 0.99 on the 25th**. It is known from the day's first minute. `dayCover` is `1 − smoothstep(0.4, 0.82, share)`, the mapping `clarity` already has. `cover` is the day's where the sky is a recorded one and round 4's gated reading where it is not. The day rolls at midnight, the rig eases the cover at its old rate (2.5 a second of the picture), so the cover leaves the day before's value over the night, where the sky cannot show it (its cover is weighted by its day factor: none under −8° of sun). A clear morning cannot be a cover: the ridge's beam is not read. `site` reads `rig.cover` as it did and follows.
