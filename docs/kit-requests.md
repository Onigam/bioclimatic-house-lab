# Kit requests (what `@onigam/lab-kit` 0.6.0 lacks, from the physics of Bioclimatic House Lab)

Numbered, one paragraph each. The kit was not patched; each item says what the lab does instead. Panel and 3D requests are numbered on, from 7 (the 3D modeler's).

1. **`stackDraft` at altitude.** `sim/thermal`'s `stackDraft(H, Tin, Tbeside)` calls `airRho` at sea-level pressure. At Odeillo, 1,550 m and 84 kPa, the draft comes out 20.6 % too high (tested in `src/sim/figures.test.ts`). This lab writes its own `draftAt(H, Tcolumn, Tbeside, p)` in `src/sim/plant.ts`. Asked: an optional pressure argument, `stackDraft(H, Tin, Tbeside, p = P_ATM)`, the same for `FILM_VERTICAL`: natural convection goes as ρ^(2/3), which this lab applies by hand. Sand, mass heater and solar heater labs are all near sea level and would see no change.
2. **`solar.ts` in the kit.** Half of this lab's physics is the sun, and the kit has none. `src/sim/solar.ts` is a copy of Solar Heater Lab's `src/sim/thermal/solar.ts`, credited, the second lab to carry it. Two additions worth moving with it: `clearSkyPlane` takes the site's altitude (Laue's correction: Meinel's formula alone is 10 to 20 % low at 1,550 m), and a coarse horizon that cuts the beam. `src/sim/optics.ts`, Fresnel on N sheets with a table per glazing, is generic too.
3. **A seeded `rng` outside three.js.** `rng` lives in `@onigam/lab-kit/three`, which the physics must not import. `src/sim/climate.ts` has its own mulberry32, as Solar Heater Lab does. Asked: export it from `@onigam/lab-kit/sim`.
4. **`tubeFlow`'s reversed flow: nothing needed, for the record.** 0.6.0 reads a negative `mdot` as laminar whatever its speed; the kit's next release fixes it with `Math.abs(mdot)` (`lab-kit/docs/sim-thermal.md`). This lab has a reversed flow every clear night, and passes `tubeFlow` the flow's size only, with the sign kept by the loop's own balance (`Plant.readFilms`, `src/sim/earth-tube.ts`), so it gives the same answer on 0.6.0 and after the fix: a test holds it. No bump needed.
5. **A `tubeFriction` that doesn't jump.** Its jump at Re 2300 (documented) is harmless in a forced flow. In a loop driven by a fraction of a pascal the same jump can pin the flow at the transition and print a false plateau, which is precisely the question here (the source's flat-topped curves). `src/sim/plant.ts` uses the larger of 64/Re and Petukhov's instead. Asked: an exported continuous variant, for loops. A question to the kit's owner rather than a need.
6. **Speed labels and a long clock.** Sand Lab's and Solar Heater Lab's requests, still open: the strip writes `×3600`, and every lab on a calendar writes its own `day of year → 21 Jun` (`src/sim/strip.ts` here).
7. **A landscape that is not called `site`.** The kit frames a world called `site` by its authored home pose, gives it camera planes of 0.1 and 4,000 m and a camera that stays above the ground (`Stage.setActive`, `Stage.frameAll`). The research calls this lab's setting `plateau`; to have those three things the 3D names it `site` (label « Le plateau », its parts keep the research's ids), and asks the far plane through `SceneModule.far` (12,000 m: the sky dome and ridges). Asked: a `SceneDef.landscape: true`, or a `SceneModule.landscape`, instead of a name, so that a lab's world ids can be its own. Mass Heater Lab (`homestead`) and Sand Lab hit the same wall and wrote their workarounds in `src/lab.ts`.
8. **A slidable section.** The research asks for a house « cut along a section you can slide »; the kit's `cutaway` is a boolean. The 3D cuts at one fixed plane (through the middle of the sample collector bay, the east half taken away, `dims.ts`: `CUT_X`), which is the section S1's Fig. 1 draws. Asked: `ViewOpts.cutPosition` (0 to 1) and a slider next to the tool, so that a visitor can walk the plane through the room, the wall and the ducts.
9. **What the world is told of the selection and of the camera.** `SceneModule.update(state, dt, view)` gets neither. The `room` world's walls step aside by the side the camera is on (`scene.onBeforeRender`, which sees the camera), and the earth tube's three parts must leave the overview's frame when the scenario is off (the 3D hides them through `registry.setDisplay`, every frame, which fights isolate and X-ray a little). Asked: `ViewOpts.camera` (position) and `ViewOpts.selected`, and a `PartRegistry.setAvailable(id, false)` that takes a part out of the overview's box and of the labels without being a display state (kit request 14 of the other labs asks for the selection).
10. **Labels for what is not a part.** The sun's two arcs of the `site` world have a label each (« December sun », « June sun »), and the kit's `Hotspot` needs a `partId`: the labels point at `prototype-house`, which is a lie of one click. Asked: a `Hotspot.partId` that may be null, and then a label that selects nothing.
11. **An instanced part's box.** `PartRegistry.bounds` uses `Box3.setFromObject`, which for an `InstancedMesh` is conservative and reads the geometry's cached bounds: a lab that makes a geometry again (the visitor moves the wall's thickness) must reset `boundingBox` and `boundingSphere` by hand (`src/three/slots.ts`). Asked: the registry to reset them itself, or a note in `docs/3d.md` of the kit.
12. **Helpers the labs copy.** `uvMetric` (UVs in metres on any geometry), `joined`, `HeatStrip` (a one-pixel texture of the model's temperatures on the kit's ramp), the section's `ClipSet` and `Caps` (`src/three/section.ts`), the procedural maps (`textures.ts`) and the instancing of repeated parts (nine collector bays, 63 heliostats, a thousand trees: one draw call each) are the sand lab's, the mass heater lab's and this lab's, copied again. Asked, as in every lab's list: the kit to carry them.
