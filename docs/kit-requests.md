# Kit requests (what `@onigam/lab-kit` 0.6.0 lacks, from the physics of Bioclimatic House Lab)

Numbered, one paragraph each. The kit was not patched; each item says what the lab does instead. Panel and 3D requests come later, from their roles.

1. **`stackDraft` at altitude.** `sim/thermal`'s `stackDraft(H, Tin, Tbeside)` calls `airRho` at sea-level pressure. At Odeillo, 1,550 m and 84 kPa, the draft comes out 20.6 % too high (tested in `src/sim/figures.test.ts`). This lab writes its own `draftAt(H, Tcolumn, Tbeside, p)` in `src/sim/plant.ts`. Asked: an optional pressure argument, `stackDraft(H, Tin, Tbeside, p = P_ATM)`, the same for `FILM_VERTICAL`: natural convection goes as ρ^(2/3), which this lab applies by hand. Sand, mass heater and solar heater labs are all near sea level and would see no change.
2. **`solar.ts` in the kit.** Half of this lab's physics is the sun, and the kit has none. `src/sim/solar.ts` is a copy of Solar Heater Lab's `src/sim/thermal/solar.ts`, credited, the second lab to carry it. Two additions worth moving with it: `clearSkyPlane` takes the site's altitude (Laue's correction: Meinel's formula alone is 10 to 20 % low at 1,550 m), and a coarse horizon that cuts the beam. `src/sim/optics.ts`, Fresnel on N sheets with a table per glazing, is generic too.
3. **A seeded `rng` outside three.js.** `rng` lives in `@onigam/lab-kit/three`, which the physics must not import. `src/sim/climate.ts` has its own mulberry32, as Solar Heater Lab does. Asked: export it from `@onigam/lab-kit/sim`.
4. **`tubeFlow`'s reversed flow: nothing needed, for the record.** 0.6.0 reads a negative `mdot` as laminar whatever its speed; the kit's next release fixes it with `Math.abs(mdot)` (`lab-kit/docs/sim-thermal.md`). This lab has a reversed flow every clear night, and passes `tubeFlow` the flow's size only, with the sign kept by the loop's own balance (`Plant.readFilms`, `src/sim/earth-tube.ts`), so it gives the same answer on 0.6.0 and after the fix: a test holds it. No bump needed.
5. **A `tubeFriction` that doesn't jump.** Its jump at Re 2300 (documented) is harmless in a forced flow. In a loop driven by a fraction of a pascal the same jump can pin the flow at the transition and print a false plateau, which is precisely the question here (the source's flat-topped curves). `src/sim/plant.ts` uses the larger of 64/Re and Petukhov's instead. Asked: an exported continuous variant, for loops. A question to the kit's owner rather than a need.
6. **Speed labels and a long clock.** Sand Lab's and Solar Heater Lab's requests, still open: the strip writes `×3600`, and every lab on a calendar writes its own `day of year → 21 Jun` (`src/sim/strip.ts` here).
