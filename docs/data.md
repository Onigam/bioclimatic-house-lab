# Data of the Bioclimatic House lab (data role)

The texts of the lab, in French and English written together: 56 part sheets, the assembly guide (20 steps) and the installation guide (10), four overviews, seven lessons with fourteen questions, the Sources panel, the interface strings. The house drawn is the 1967 CNRS prototype at Odeillo; its source is the 1976 paper of the team that built it (S1 of `docs/research.md`), so the lab has **no main video**. Every number comes from `docs/research.md`, `docs/physics.md` or `src/three/dims.ts`.

## Conventions

- **Marks.** No mark: reported by a source (named in words: « l'article », « les inventeurs »). **†**: computed, from reported figures (research: derived) or by the model. **\***: estimated, the sources are silent, a choice of this lab. « À l'œil » (words): read off a published curve. `lf` refuses an unknown placeholder at import; `figures.ts` decides the mark in one place (`markOf`). The legend is said once, in the overview of the first world, and on each sheet that carries dimensions.
- **Said once.** « Educational reconstruction », the safety line, the hazards together, and that rules differ by country and site: the Sources panel (`safety`, `hazards`, `disclaimer`, `legal`, `LAW`). « Not a plan to follow »: the first step of each guide. Each warning where the gesture is risky, in the step's `warning`: the weight (assembly `storage-wall`), the glass at height (`glazing`), the mains (`heating`), the vents shut in summer (installation `summer-position`), fresh air and carbon monoxide (`fresh-air`). **That the earth tube is a what-if**: the overview of `house` and the pipe's sheet. **That the plateau is composed and that the sun's arcs, the heliostats and the snow follow the model**: the overview of `site`. **Hygiene and radon of an earth tube**: lesson 7. A test counts each.
- **No « this is a model » on a sheet**: the simulation panel says it once (`MODEL_NOTE`).
- **Tools.** A tip names only a tool its world has: no Section on the plateau, the wall and the room, no Explode or Slow-mo on the plateau; isolating hides what is around, so no tip asks to look at it.
- **Names.** Félix Trombe, Jacques Michel (the architect, by Mérimée), D. Kelbaugh, B. Anderson, J. D. Balcomb appear as the sources give them. An American architect stands for Kelbaugh on the sheets; the 1974 building's owners are never named.
- **Nothing says where the houses stand beyond « Odeillo »** (a test over the content and these documents; the street of the monument, the coordinates and the identifiers never appear). The open data's outline is credited « sans la situer ». The `docplayer` link to the 2011 protection dossier is **not** reproduced, because its address holds the street.
- **English** follows British spelling: draught, metre, grey, insulation; « the », « a house of 1967 ».
- **Numbers.** A reported figure is typed in both spellings and held by `src/data/reported.ts` (each group says which table of the research it is). Any other goes through `figures.ts`. A test compares the numbers of each text in French and in English one by one (an English 10-at-night is the French 22 h).

## The estimates, as the sheets mark them

The frame's section (22 mm), its glazing bars and four panes to a column; the 25 mm between the sheets; the 0.10 m of the lower duct above the floor; the air probes' heights (15, 50 and 85 % of the span) and the thermocouples' depths (14, 50, 150, 300, 450, 585 mm); the plan of the rooms, the partitions (0.10 m), the doors, the living room's four bays; the roof's build-up (rafters 0.14 m, covering 0.03 m, insulation 0.10 m laid over the ceiling), the overhang, the ridge height, the pitch (rough); the chimney's section and place; the foundation's footing (0.5 m), its five steps and the ground's fall; the floor slab (0.20 m), its tile, the ceiling (0.06 m); the window, the recess's door and transom, the balcony and its stair, the head band; the north window; the heater, the panel and the recorder (type and size); the fresh-air vent (nothing is drawn behind it but the earth tube's last run, in the what-if); the flap's mechanism; the furnace's mirror outline and the 1974 building's plan (only the footprints and heights are open data); the twin's offset; the species of the trees; the road; the granite; and, for the model, 16.7 m² of windows, 500 W of people, a paint absorptance of 0.93, loop losses K = 4, a room mass of 20 MJ/K, a wind of 2 m/s, the earth tube's 50 m, Ø 200 mm, 2 m, 150 m³/h and 1.5 % of fall.

2. **The pyranometer's plane.** The research names a Kipp pyranometer « at the laboratory » and does not say how it was oriented, so the sheet says only what the paper counts. To read on the page.
3. **Whether S1's « 1967 prototypes » are one design twice** (S5, searched only): the twin's sheet says the paper speaks of prototypes and nothing more. Open question 3 of the research (the IGN outline) is stated on that sheet and in the Sources, flagged, without a place.
4. **What the paper's letters T_s and V mean** (figure 1): the head and sill sheets say they are read as a head and a foot, our reading.
5. **How fresh air came in; the chimney's use; the heater's type**: unknown; the sheets say so, the vent has nothing behind it but the what-if earth tube's last run.
6. **How the houses changed position, and when**: the paper does not say; the model's June is run in the summer position by choice.
7. **The monument's plan** and the **protection dossier** (S5): unread, the host does not answer; the Sources say so (history, not law). Research question 6 (writing to the laboratory and to the heritage authority) is the owner's.
8. **The video question (research §8, question 4).** The roadmap's film that sells a document and says 1956 and 20 cm **is not credited**, as the research recommends; Penn Ar Web's film is credited as a companion we have **not watched** (title and channel only: its date and licence are unverified), no fact taken from it, and it is not recommended. If the owner keeps the first, a line goes to `sources.ts` and to nothing else.
9. **The title (question 9)** is the research's proposal, awaiting the owner; applied as a **reversible default** at integration in `public/lab.json` (title; tagline = the fuller showcase line above, as the sister labs do), `index.html`, `package.json` and the README; the top bar's `labTitle` and `tagline` strings are shorter (see above).
10. **The architect's earth-tube figures** (10 and 17 °C) are left out: the transcript is Whisper's, numbers to check by ear. The lesson says only that an architect, in a video we could only transcribe roughly, says he has never fitted one (fact-check, fix 12); the figures stay estimates, said so.

## What the fact-check and the review changed (2026-11-11)

Sentences, links and the Sources panel only; no figure of the model moved (`docs/fact-check.md`, `docs/qa.md`).

- **Attributions.** The question « can a house heat itself? » is the lab's and the film's, not the inventors' (their paper states a result). A calculation of ours is ours (the 31 W/m² by hand); the ± 28 % is the paper's uncertainty on the heat the air carries, not a margin on the flow and not a blame of turbulence; « 36.7 % over 20 days » is our sum of the paper's own rows, which print no total (`eff20Paper`, †); the 1974 wall's 8 to 10 points have **two causes in the paper** (a smaller exchange area and the water in the concrete, expected to go in a year or two), and « cast in place » is not the paper's; the people are not said to be counted by the paper; Kelbaugh's reverse flow leaves warm by the upper vents and returns cooler by the lower ones; the emissivity 0.94 is the paper's for the room's surfaces, taken again for the paint as an estimate.
- **The anemometer.** The paper puts it at the centre of the lower duct, and so does the 3D now (the sheet, the guide step and the tip say so). The paper's air thermocouples sit in the ducts; our three probes hang in the gap, and the sheets say they are ours.
- **The earth tube and the room's fixtures.** The tube's three parts are always drawn (pale while the scenario is off, firm when it runs), buried in a window cut in the ground north of the house, as five parallel runs ending at the north wall's fresh-air vent; the route is our drawing. What hangs on a room wall goes with it when the camera is outside it. The east gable is a pale ghost with its edges while the cut is on, plain when it is off.
- **Bases.** « No windows 42 % » is the windows replaced by plain wall with the people at 500 W; the « wall alone 32 % » keeps the windows' loss and removes only their sun.
- **Said as reported, not as read.** A simulation of 1978 (« of the order of 40 % ») is unread; the earth-tube video's architect is « transcribed roughly »; the companion film is title and channel only, not recommended.
- **Places.** The owner's rule (no place beyond « Odeillo ») now holds for the commune, its département, the regions, the département's main city (the model's sea-level site is « at sea level »; its identifier is `sealevel`), a villa's town and owner, and every record of the national heritage database (cited by its home page; the facts keep their dates). The Sources' labels are French and English. `src/guard.test.ts` and `src/narration.test.ts` fail if one comes back, and `docs/research.md` and `docs/physics.md` were redacted to match.
- **Register.** The installation guide tells what the inventors did and what the model finds, not what to do; the scenario « Isoler d'abord » is « La même maison, mieux isolée »; the law table has one line, « rules differ by country and site: ask your local authority ».
- **The stair** has one count, `STAIR_STEPS` of `dims.ts`: the 3D builds it, PL-H01 counts it, the balcony's sheet prints it (`FIG.stairSteps`).

## The lab is international (owner's decision, after the second review)

The channel and the labs are international, so the lab carries **no national text of law or regulation** that nobody can check in a primary text. Where it says that rules exist (radon, protected buildings, thermal regulation, electrical work, building codes) it says « les règles varient selon le pays et le site : voyez votre autorité locale » / « rules differ by country and site: ask your local authority », once in the law table and in the Sources' legal notice, and in lesson 7's radon paragraph (radon is a ground gas that can collect in spaces in contact with the earth, a buried pipe included: where it is a concern, an airtight pipe and a measurement). Gone from the Sources: the radon order and the radon thematic page, the Heritage Code, the 1982 ventilation order and the RE2020 entry; gone from the law table: the French lines, the panorama exception, the « elsewhere » line. Kept: the heritage facts of the real buildings as history (the 1974 building and the furnace, with their dates, the building credited by the national database's home page), and the data licences. The furnace's three sources (a press release, a photograph's captions, an encyclopaedia article) print its address, its coordinates or its commune: they are credited by title and year, with no link. `src/guard.test.ts` fails on a link to any of those hosts and on any national text in the app.
