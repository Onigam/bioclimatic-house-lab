# Water

There is no open water in this lab: no pond, stream, canal, tank or pipe that carries a liquid. Nothing in the four worlds is drawn as water. What the sky does with water (snow on the ground, a few clouds) is listed in `docs/scenery.md`, and the snow's amount follows the model's climate there. The earth tube's drain pit is drawn dry: the model has no condensation (`docs/physics.md` §9).
