## Summary

**22 defects: 4 blockers, 7 majors, 11 minors.** The gates are green (typecheck, format, 673 tests, build) and the standard's list of shortfalls is empty; the 122 clips of the spoken guide play to the end in both languages with no error and the 3D follows them. What stops this lab is not what it lacks but four things it shows:

1. **D1, privacy**: lesson 7 names the commune in full, with its radon zone, and the Sources panel links to a town hall, to a département's radon map and to the heritage notice of the 1974 building, whose page prints the building's street address and plot reference, under a panel that says « nous ne désignons aucune maison au-delà d'Odeillo ». 2. **D2, D3, D6, things that float**: the earth tube's what-if is drawn through the glass front and lies on the snow from above; the room's heater, panel, window and door hang in the air as soon as the camera leaves the room; isolating a part leaves the coat, the stool, the furniture hanging in the void.
3. **D4, the camera in the plateau** does not turn around a selected part (the owner's Pelton remark of 28/10), and **D5**, the zoom still pops in three worlds (3.4, 4.6, 4.7 against a limit of 2), as the integrator's notes said.

All four of the integrator's weak points are confirmed (D8, D2, D5, D12). The narrator's two sheet defects and its cut tips are confirmed in the rendered panels (D11, D7). No promise of a result for a reader's own house was found (D21 is about register).

### 4. Does the lab promise a result for a reader's own house, or read as design advice or a regulation guide?

No promise of an energy saving, a comfort, a payback or a cost for a reader's own house anywhere: the figures are model results (†), estimates (*), reported ones attributed to a source, and the lab says it explains and gives no plan (every sheet's title block, the first step of both guides, the Sources). The only saving quoted is « 72 % économisés » by one named house at Princeton, and the lab says its rooms were kept cooler than 20 °C. The honest answer to the film's question, « une bonne part, selon qui compte, et quoi », is repeated in lesson 5, the panel and the guide. ## Verdict

**Not ready for the owner's checkpoint.** On the evidence of this review the lab has a standing-rule violation that the test suite cannot see (D1: a commune named in a lesson, links to pages that carry a département, a region and a street address, and a sentence claiming the opposite), and three families of floating things that the owner has called out on every lab since Pelton (the earth tube's what-if, the room's fixtures from outside, the props left after an isolate), plus the plateau's camera, the pops in the zoom and the house that is small on a phone. None of the 22 is deep: the privacy items are texts and link labels (a general sentence in `lessons.ts`, three entries in `sources.ts`, the place select of `sim/texts.ts`, which is a decision for you about whether the comparison towns count), the floating items are visibility rules in three scene files (`earthtube.ts`, `room/scene.ts`, `wall/livedin.ts` and the furniture), the cut tips and the sheet sentences are one-line text edits, and the stair is one constant on a sheet. Once those are done I would expect a second review to find a lab that can go to you; what already holds: the model, the sheets, the guides, the 56 part sheets, the lessons and the spoken guide's mechanics are in good order (0 console errors in 39 runs, 122 clips matched to their subtitles, nothing left on, no text overflow at 1280 px), and the 1974 building and the furnace read as composed. The strip (D10), the phone pose (D8) and the guide's timing want a look on a real device, which I could not do. I have not set any status; that is yours.
