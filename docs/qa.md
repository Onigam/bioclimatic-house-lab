## Summary

What stops this lab is not what it lacks but four things it shows:

1. **D1, privacy**: lesson 7 names the commune in full, with its radon zone, and the Sources panel links to a town hall, to a département's radon map and to the heritage notice of the 1974 building, whose page prints the building's street address and plot reference, under a panel that says « nous ne désignons aucune maison au-delà d'Odeillo ». 3. **D4, the camera in the plateau** does not turn around a selected part (the owner's Pelton remark of 28/10), and **D5**, the zoom still pops in three worlds (3.4, 4.6, 4.7 against a limit of 2), as the integrator's notes said.

All four of the integrator's weak points are confirmed (D8, D2, D5, D12). The narrator's two sheet defects and its cut tips are confirmed in the rendered panels (D11, D7). No promise of a result for a reader's own house was found (D21 is about register).

### 4. Does the lab promise a result for a reader's own house, or read as design advice or a regulation guide?

No promise of an energy saving, a comfort, a payback or a cost for a reader's own house anywhere: the figures are model results (†), estimates (*), reported ones attributed to a source, and the lab says it explains and gives no plan (every sheet's title block, the first step of both guides, the Sources). The only saving quoted is « 72 % économisés » by one named house at Princeton, and the lab says its rooms were kept cooler than 20 °C. The honest answer to the film's question, « une bonne part, selon qui compte, et quoi », is repeated in lesson 5, the panel and the guide.
