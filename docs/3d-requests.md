## Settled at integration (the lead, 2026-11-11)

1. **The ground's fall: 2.0 m.** The research gives « about 2–3.5 m across the footprint » (open data, plan precision 3 m); `docs/3d.md` and the comment on `dims.ts` both called the estimate the low end, 2.0 m, while the constants made 1.7 m, under the range. `GROUND_SOUTH` is now `GROUND_NORTH − 2.0` (−2.3 m; the floor stays 0.3 m above the ground at the north wall), the eaves stand 6.5 m over the south ground, inside the open data's 5.8 to 6.7 m. The sheets read `groundFall` from `dims.ts` and now print 2.0 m\*; the balcony's stair, which follows the ground, has 14 treads (the sheet said « de l'ordre de dix »: it now says « une quinzaine »). `dims.test.ts` holds the fall and the eaves.
2. **The north window's `x`**: `ROOM.northWindow.x = −0.6` (and `ROOM.freshVent.x = 1.55`, the same duplicate), read by the shell, the heater (which stands under the window) and the vent; the number was typed in four places. PL-H01 now draws the north window in plan, with its label, from that `x`. `dims.test.ts` checks both clear of the gables and of each other.
3. **The chimney's height.** `CHIMNEY.above` is measured from the **ridge line** (`Y_RIDGE`) to the top of the stack, under its cap and flue pot: that is what the 3D builds, and what `machine.test.ts` now holds to 3 mm (it only checked « over 0.6 m »). PL-H01's elevation read it from the roof's surface at the stack's place, 0.4 m lower; it now reads `Y_RIDGE + CHIMNEY.above`.
