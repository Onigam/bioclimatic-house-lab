# Fact-check, Bioclimatic House Lab

Independent check of the lab's public text against its sources, in French and English. Lab at local `main` 45c7071 (clean tree). Written 2026-11-11. I wrote none of the lab. No file of the lab was edited other than this report; my throwaway scripts, the downloaded papers and a copy of the lab's `src/` and `docs/` (to run its tests) live in the session scratchpad.

**Verdict: not ready for the owner to read.** Six rows are wrong as written (one attributes a calculation of ours to the authors, one puts an instrument in the wrong duct, one gives the Kelbaugh reverse flow backwards, one credits the inventors with a question they never asked), one claim has no support and contradicts the lab's own lesson, 42 rows overreach their source (several merge two passages of one source, the fault found in the gasifier lab), and the place rule (« Odeillo and nothing else ») is broken in the lab's own text, links and labels listed in section 4. The figures themselves are right: every number I could reproduce (S1's tables, PVGIS, the IGN footprints, the model's own results) matched. The faults are in the sentences around them.
