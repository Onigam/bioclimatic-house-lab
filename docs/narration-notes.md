# Notes of the spoken guide's writer, for the lead

What was found while writing `narration/fr.json` and `narration/en.json` that is not the narrator's to fix (`src/three`, `src/sim`, the sheets and the lessons are untouched). The guide itself is described in `narration/README.md`.

## Text defects in the sheets

1. **`air-gap`, « Conception »** (`src/data/parts/wall.ts`): « le modèle prend 4\*, au milieu de de 3 à 5\* » and « in the middle of from 3 to 5\* ». `lossKRange` already carries « de » and « from », so the sentence has it twice. Say « au milieu de la fourchette, 3 à 5\* », or drop the word from the sentence.
2. **`west-gable`, « Description »** (`src/data/parts/house.ts`): « la fondation descend en 5 gradins\* gradins sous lui » and « steps\* steps beneath it »: `gableSteps` carries its unit, the sentence adds the noun again.
3. **The tips « Activez la Coupe »** (`south-facade`, `floor-slab`): the house opens cut (« Ce monde s'ouvre coupé », the first frame turns the section on), so on a first visit the tool is already on and « Activez » reads as an order to switch it off. The tips of `entrance-balcony` and `west-gable` already say « Gardez la Coupe active »; the same wording would do here. `east-gable` says « Activez la Coupe : ce pignon part avec la moitié est » and the gable is in fact visible only without the cut: « Coupez, puis remettez l'ensemble » is nearer the truth, or « Désactivez la Coupe pour le voir ».

## Third pass (11 Nov, the second fact-check, `docs/fact-check.md` section 7)

The second pass found the first one holding (48 of 54 rows fixed everywhere, 5 partly, 1 unchanged) and five new small faults, three of them in the voice. Eight clips per language were redone, locally.

- **N1** (`fresh-air-vent`): the 3D and the earth-tube sheet now end the tube at the north wall's grille, so « behind it… nothing: we draw neither duct nor intake » was false whenever the tube is drawn (always, pale). Now « et derrière elle… rien, sauf dans l'hypothèse du puits canadien, dont le dernier tronçon finit ici » / « and behind it… nothing, except in the what-if earth tube, whose last run ends here ». « Hypothèse » stands for the report's « et si », whose quotation marks the voice cannot say; it is `scene-house`'s own word. The cues did not move.
- **N4** (`intro`): « the others come from our simulation or our estimates » was too wide (the sun and air data are open data; the IGN figures are too). Now « The others come from open data, our simulation or our estimates: none was measured by us » / « Les autres viennent de données ouvertes, de notre simulation ou de nos estimations : aucun n'a été mesuré par nous ». « Calculations, not measurements » is gone with it: open data are not calculations. « Our arithmetic » (the report's fourth source, for the derived 63 tonnes and fifteen hours) is not named; the sheets mark those figures.
- **N5** (`room-surface-probes`): the seventeen and a half is worked out from the paper's figures, not read on a curve: « From the paper's figures, we work out about seventeen and a half » / « Avec les chiffres de l'article, nous calculons environ dix-sept et demi ».
- **The partly fixed items** (section 7.2: the 1978 paper's forty per cent, the earth-tube video's sentence, « higher up », the ground's fall of about two metres). The voice never carried the first two (no « 1978 », no forty per cent, no architect who never fitted a tube; the architect of `north-window` is the American builder of the second source). It did carry the fall: `granite-slope` now says « about two metres: an estimate » (the sheet: the low end of the range the open data give, an estimate), and, for the same reason, `earth-tube-pipe` says its fifty metres and two metres are « our estimates » (the sheets star them and say they come from an architect's illustration in a video nobody could reach). « Higher up the slope » (`scene-site`, `solar-houses-1974`) is unchanged, as in the sheets, which do not hedge it either; the report lists it as unreachable and unchanged.
- **Budget**: about 105 characters added, three sentences cut that the sheets or another segment carry (« Aucune hypothèse n'est lue » in `chimney`, « Il le dit, il ne tranche pas » in `electric-heater`, « Un souci de conception, avant d'être un souci de lavage » in `inner-glazing`): 23,958 characters in French, 22,869 in English.
- **N2, N3, the black-paint spec row**: sheets and Sources, not the voice.
